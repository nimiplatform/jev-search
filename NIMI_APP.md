# Jev Search

Search the web in plain language and get ranked links and snippets. Jev Search helps select sources, search terms and time ranges, and shows relevance judgments instead of generating an answer.

## Start searching

Open Jev Search from Nimi Home. Use the key button in the App's settings to enter your own Search1API key, and configure the Nimi text-decision capability used to interpret and rank searches.

Enter a request such as “Rust async runtimes on Hacker News this month”. Review and adjust the source and time filters, then inspect the returned links. A failed source is reported separately from a source with no results.

## Data and limits

Web searches use Search1API and require network access and your service account. AI judgments use your selected Nimi resource; Cloud judgments send the relevant query or result text to the chosen service. Search-service charges and AI charges depend on your configuration.

Results can be incomplete or incorrectly ranked. Open the original pages before relying on a snippet. For help, contact the App provider with the search filters and error; never include your API key.
